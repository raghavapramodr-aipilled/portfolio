People. Pixels. Percussion.

Hey, I am Raghava Pramod.

I hire people for a living. The rest of the time, I'm usually taking photos on my phone: at traffic lights, on lunch breaks, or wherever I end up on a weekend. If a shape or a bit of light catches my eye, I shoot it. When I need to switch off, I play the tabla or the cajón. This site is where I keep the photos I'm proud of, with a few words on how I took them.
