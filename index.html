People. Pixels. Percussion.

Hey, I'm Raghava.

By day I hire people for a living. In between, while waiting at traffic lights, during lunch breaks, or wherever I end up on a weekend, I'm usually shooting something on my phone because a shape or a bit of light caught my eye. When I need to switch my brain off completely, I sit down with a tabla or a cajón and play. This site is where I keep the shots I'm proud of and talk a bit about why I took them the way I did.
